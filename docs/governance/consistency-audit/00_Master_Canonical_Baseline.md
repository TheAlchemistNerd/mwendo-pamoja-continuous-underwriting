## Purpose, scope, and authority

This baseline is the controlling reconciliation standard for the ten Mwendo Pamoja target documents. It was prepared after semantic comparison of the three `question drafts`, the four `regulatory_tech_kesonia` papers, and target documents D01 through D10. `glossary.md` was excluded and is neither evidence nor a target. The baseline does not amend any source document. It identifies the propositions that a future coordinated redraft should use unless transaction counsel, the Central Bank of Kenya, an accounting adviser, a signed commercial agreement, or validated portfolio data establishes a stronger answer.

Authority is applied in this order: Kenyan law and official regulator material; applicable accounting and prudential standards; executed contracts and lender terms; validated empirical evidence; approved internal policy; and finally illustrative assumptions. A statement from a draft, a vendor page, or a professional article cannot convert a policy preference into law. A model output cannot become a contractual guarantee. Every material number must carry its currency, denominator, period, source status, and as-of date.

## Canonical entity and transaction model

Mwendo Pamoja HoldCo owns intellectual property, employs or contracts the core technology and governance team, and licenses services to operating and financing entities. OpCo, if retained, operates customer, platform, and carrier integrations and performs only the functions assigned to it under executed agreements. The bankruptcy-remote SPV purchases eligible receivables under a legally effective sale and applies collections under a lender-controlled waterfall. The bank or licensed credit provider originates or funds credit only to the extent permitted by its licence and agreements. The insurance carrier underwrites and issues insurance contracts. A technology company or MGA must not be called the carrier unless it holds the required Kenyan authority. The mobility platform supplies agreed data and payment-collection services, but it does not automatically owe fiduciary duties or grant routing control.

Bankruptcy remoteness is a legal and operational result, not an ERP configuration. It requires true-sale analysis, perfection, separateness covenants, restrictions on additional debt, independent governance where required, non-petition and limited-recourse provisions, account control, servicing continuity, commingling controls, and legal opinions. Posting separate ledgers in Dynamics 365 is useful evidence of separateness but cannot create it.

## Canonical financing baseline

The primary SPV capitalization is USD 9.00 million equivalent, reported to investors in USD but operated and settled in KES unless definitive financing documents choose otherwise. The optional HoldCo facility is USD 1.00 million. The consolidated funding view is therefore USD 10.00 million. HoldCo operating cash flow is excluded from the SPV waterfall.

The SPV stack is:

| Class | Amount | Share | Economic position |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Class A | USD 6.75 million | 75% | Senior secured notes |
| Class B | USD 1.35 million | 15% | Subordinated or mezzanine notes |
| Class C | USD 0.90 million | 10% | First-loss equity or residual certificate |
| Total | USD 9.00 million | 100% | Total SPV capitalization |

Class percentages describe the capital stack. They are not, by themselves, collateral advance rates. The borrowing-base advance rate must be defined separately as funded amount divided by eligible receivables. The controlling collateral ratio is:

```text
OC ratio = eligible receivables / (Class A outstanding + Class B outstanding)
```

The minimum OC ratio is 125%, subject to precise eligibility, delinquency, concentration, dilution, and haircut rules. If a 75% total-capital advance assumption is retained, USD 9.00 million divided by 75% implies USD 12.00 million of eligible receivables. At closing, USD 12.00 million divided by USD 8.10 million of funded debt produces 148.15% debt-note OC. Both calculations must be displayed because they answer different questions. No document may label the 75% Class A share, the total-capital purchase rate, and the borrowing-base advance rate as the same metric.

The cash reserve account target is three months of defined senior and mezzanine debt service. The definition must state whether it includes scheduled principal, interest, trustee costs, hedge payments, and servicing transition costs. Reserve cash does not hedge FX merely because it is cash. Any USD liability against KES assets requires an explicit natural hedge, cross-currency hedge, or quantified open position.

The waterfall is taxes and trustee expenses where legally senior, servicing and backup servicing, Class A interest, Class A principal according to schedule and sweeps, Class B interest, Class B principal, reserve replenishment, and residual distributions. Definitive documents must specify how recoveries, insurance refunds, principal, interest, fees, hedge receipts, and liquidation proceeds are characterized. Equity distributions stop during an OC, reserve, payment, concentration, model-governance, or early-amortization breach where the covenant so provides.

## KESONIA, customer pricing, and note pricing

As of 24 August 2026, KESONIA is the transaction-based Kenya shilling overnight unsecured interbank average administered by CBK. CBK began publication on 1 September 2025. Under the revised risk-based credit pricing model, new variable-rate KES loans transitioned from 1 September 2025 and existing variable-rate KES loans by 28 February 2026. Fixed-rate and foreign-currency facilities are outside that specific variable-rate formulation. CBR is an alternative where KESONIA is impractical or unavailable, subject to the applicable CBK framework and contract [1]-[3].

Customer pricing is represented as:

```text
Total lending rate = KESONIA + K_RBCP
Total cost of credit = KESONIA + K_RBCP + fees and charges
```

`K_RBCP` includes costs related to lending, shareholder return, and the borrower's risk profile. It is not limited to expected credit loss. There is no additional generic `Bank Margin` outside `K_RBCP` unless the contract and disclosure framework use a different authorised structure. The model must disclose components without double counting.

SPV note pricing is separate from customer pricing. The canonical Class A basis is compounded KESONIA in arrears plus negotiated margin `m_A`. Class B uses compounded KESONIA plus `m_B`, or a fixed negotiated coupon if the term sheet so states. CBR is documented as the fallback where appropriate. The 91-day T-bill is a legacy or comparison benchmark unless definitive lender terms elect it. A note coupon does not equal asset yield, funds-transfer price, WACC, or the investor discount rate.

The controlled notation is:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `K_RBCP` | Customer pricing premium under the CBK risk-based framework |
| `K_cap` | Regulatory capital amount or ratio, always with units |
| `m_A`, `m_B` | Contractual Class A and Class B margins |
| `EL` | Expected credit loss for the stated horizon and measure |
| `CoC` | Cost of capital assumption |
| `OpEx` | Allocated operating cost |
| `PD_P` | Real-world probability of default |
| `PD_Q` | Risk-neutral probability used only in a supported valuation overlay |

Compounding must specify calendar, day-count basis, publication lag, reset period, observation shift or lookback, non-business-day treatment, and fallback. A five-day lookback is not described as a CBK mandate without official authority. SQL implementations must store rates as decimals, use the contractual day-count basis, weight every applicable calendar day exactly once, and preserve the rate vintage used at calculation time.

## Canonical underwriting and feature architecture

The architecture is:

```text
Raw telematics and wallet events
             |
      Flink feature services
        /              \
GRU/Transformer      Explicit Liquidity
neural embeddings    Feature Path
        \              /
 Hierarchical Bayesian underwriting
             |
 Credit Policy and Compliance Gate
```

The Explicit Liquidity Feature Path is the low-latency Kappa and Redis serving and computation path. It calculates interpretable variables such as wallet cash-flow asymmetry, dynamic debt-to-liquidity ratio, Earnings Velocity, Repayment Velocity, wallet volatility, reserve balance, time since depletion, utilisation, and defined interactions. These engineered metrics bypass the GRU and Transformer and enter hierarchical Bayesian logistic regression directly through spline and interaction terms.

Raw wallet events may also be presented to a neural sequence model for rhythm, timing, and behavioural-state learning. The canonical engineered metrics must have one owner. CFA, DLR, Earnings Velocity, and Repayment Velocity are not simultaneously declared neural inputs. The concern is duplicate information, leakage, identifiability, and explanation integrity, not a prohibition on two branches observing the same governed raw event.

The neural branch receives high-frequency behavioural sequences and a properly represented sequence of lower-frequency context. Cross-attention is used only if keys and values contain multiple contextual tokens. If context has already been pooled to one vector, a gated fusion or concatenation layer is more honest than a one-key attention block.

The Bayesian model combines neural representation `Phi_neural` and explicit vector `Z_liquidity`:

```text
logit(PD_P) = cluster intercept
            + neural representation effect
            + spline effects(Z_liquidity)
            + explicit interactions
            + cohort and time shock effects
```

It returns posterior PD, uncertainty intervals, exceedance probabilities, and portfolio aggregates. It does not by itself produce a market-consistent `PD_Q`. A P-to-Q transformation requires observable market calibration, separation of liquidity and structural credit enhancement, and validation against relevant tradable instruments [22]. Where such data are absent, pricing must be described as real-world expected loss plus liquidity, capital, operating, and uncertainty premiums, not as risk-neutral valuation.

The downstream Credit Policy and Compliance Gate applies legal eligibility, contractual covenants, affordability and sufficiency rules, product limits, uncertainty kill-switches, concentration limits, fraud controls, reserve and OC tests, NPL triggers, and approved intervention rules. It produces approve, decline, reduce limit, freeze draw, restructure, suspend purchases, sweep cash, or block distributions. A policy gate is not a model feature and a policy threshold is not a regulation unless the regulator says so.

## Data, lineage, and system-of-record baseline

Each financial event carries event time, ingestion time, availability time, effective accounting date, source system, correction version, and lineage identifier. Flink uses event-time semantics, watermarks, late-data handling, idempotent keys, and deterministic replay. Point-in-time training joins use what was actually available at the decision time, not merely the latest corrected record. Lakehouse time travel assists reproduction but does not alone prevent leakage.

The lakehouse zones have distinct roles: Bronze retains source events; Silver contains validated, standardised, deduplicated records; Gold contains approved analytical facts and features. Apache Iceberg and Delta Lake are product choices, while Kappa, Lambda, and medallion are architectural patterns. The documents must not call an Iceberg implementation a Delta Lake product without qualification.

Dynamics 365 Finance is the accounting ERP endpoint and general-ledger system of record for approved postings. It is not natively an instrument-level banking ALM, matched-maturity FTP, loan servicing, insurance subledger, immutable evidence vault, or regulatory SupTech platform. Those functions require a purpose-built system, an ISV extension, or controlled custom services. Oracle OFSAA is cited as an example of native banking ALM and FTP capability [14], [15]. The authoritative source for each datum must be named: event store for raw telemetry, feature store for online feature state, model registry for approved models, servicing ledger for contractual balances, and ERP for statutory journals.

ISO 20022 is a controlled financial messaging standard, not permission to invent message types or XML fields. Custom regulatory payloads require an agreed schema and regulator endpoint. `auth.015`, `<RsptvRskPrm>`, `<XplnblAIVal>`, and `<EIRMod>` are not presented as verified CBK reporting fields. XBRL and ISO 20022 solve different reporting and messaging problems.

Security claims use measurable controls. AES-256-GCM provides authenticated encryption when nonce uniqueness, key management, implementation, and tag verification are correct [12]. It is not described as absolute or military-grade protection. WORM retention, digital signatures, mTLS, maker-checker approval, access logging, key rotation, incident response, and recovery are separate controls.

## Accounting, prudential risk, and governance baseline

IFRS 9 applies to financial assets and liabilities held by the relevant reporting entity. The SPV or originating lender uses real-world, forward-looking PD, LGD, EAD, scenario weights, significant-increase-in-credit-risk rules, and contractual effective interest rate mechanics. IFRS 17 applies to insurance contracts in the carrier's reporting perimeter. It does not determine SPV note capital or substitute for the carrier's applicable Kenyan prudential regime [5], [6].

The IBOR reform amendments were a narrow response to benchmark replacement and do not create a general practical expedient for routine KESONIA resets [4]. Modification accounting must consider contractual terms and IFRS 9 requirements. A 10% test is principally associated with substantial modification of financial liabilities; it is not an automatic bright line for financial assets. A one-percent NPV rule from a foreign supervisory framework is not a general IFRS 9 law in Kenya.

Basel IRB calculations apply only to banks with supervisory permission and the required data, governance, validation, and parameter standards. Prescribed asset correlations and output-floor constraints cannot be lowered merely by submitting a Clayton copula parameter. Copulas may inform internal concentration and economic-capital analysis, but they do not prove capital relief [8]. Solvency II, EBA guidance, ECOA, and Federal Reserve SR 11-7 may be useful comparative references, not binding Kenyan rules unless contractually adopted or incorporated by local authority. SR 11-7 remains a sound model-risk benchmark for independent validation, governance, inventory, monitoring, and effective challenge [9].

PSI thresholds of 0.10 and 0.25 are monitoring conventions. They may be adopted as internal or contractual triggers, but do not automatically require regulator notification, retraining, or suspension of an IRB permission. Discrimination, calibration, uncertainty, drift, fairness, and outcomes must be monitored separately. Equalized odds is one fairness criterion and can conflict with calibration; MMD or feature blindness cannot guarantee it.

## Financial-model, stress, and claim discipline

Every forecast must separate source-backed inputs, derived values, illustrative assumptions, scenario shocks, and calculated outputs. Default rates, recovery rates, yields, originations, driver counts, FX rates, tax rates, fees, capital costs, and intervention effects require source status and as-of dates. Claims such as 35% yield, 40% historical recovery, 14-day advance detection, 1.5 million drivers, or near-perfect prediction remain unverified until supported by a portfolio tape, experiment, or authoritative market source.

`Class A principal protected`, `zero risk`, `mathematically guaranteed`, and equivalent phrases are prohibited. The permitted form is conditional: under the stated scenario, model assumptions, waterfall, recovery timing, and legal enforceability, the projection shows no Class A principal loss. Tail-dependence parameters must be empirically estimated and subjected to sensitivity analysis. A Clayton copula cannot prove that contagion is contained.

WACC is a valuation discount-rate concept for an entity or project with a stated capital structure. The SPV waterfall uses contractual coupons, fees, hedge costs, taxes, and timing of distributions. These are not interchangeable. A tax shield is recognised only after confirming the SPV's tax status, deductibility, taxable income, and ability to use the shield. Class B cannot be treated as equity for tax simply because it is subordinated.

## Roadmap and decision gates

Execution begins with legal and economic architecture, data rights, licences, customer consent, carrier and platform commitments, and an agreed target operating model. Data and modelling follow only after source-system and point-in-time controls are defined. Accounting configuration follows a chart-of-accounts and subledger design approved by finance and auditors. Shadow scoring, controlled pilot, independent validation, fairness testing, security testing, servicing simulation, and waterfall reconciliation precede funding scale-up.

Each phase has an accountable owner, entry criteria, exit evidence, go or no-go decision, rollback plan, dependency register, and regulatory or contractual approval. The roadmap must include procurement, privacy impact assessment, model validation, legal opinions, backup servicing, business continuity, incident response, user acceptance, parallel financial close, and post-launch monitoring. A six-month implementation is an ambition, not a commitment, until dependencies are resourced.
