7. arXiv
arXiv is a moderated preprint repository, not a peer-reviewed journal. Submissions must be topical, refereeable scientific contributions, and first-time authors or new categories may require endorsement. Official submission guidance, endorsement guidance.
Recommended categories:
- Telematics paper: q-fin.RM as the most natural primary category, with possible cross-listing to stat.AP, stat.ME, stat.ML or cs.LG.
- Mwendo credit-method paper: q-fin.RM, potentially cross-listed to stat.ME, stat.CO or cs.LG.
- Mwendo financial-economics paper: econ.EM only after it contains genuine econometric evidence.
The arXiv taxonomy defines q-fin.RM as financial-risk measurement and management across banking, insurance and corporate applications.
I would not submit the complete six-part Mwendo white paper to arXiv unchanged. A focused, refereeable quantitative manuscript would be more likely to pass moderation and reach the right audience.
8. SSRN
I assume “SSRI” means SSRN.
SSRN is probably the best repository for the complete Mwendo white paper because it accommodates finance, law, economics, regulation and working papers better than arXiv. SSRN states that papers are classifier-reviewed before posting but are distributed before journal peer review. Authors can select up to twelve subject eJournals. SSRN author guidance.
Recommended subject families:
- FinTech.
- Banking and financial institutions.
- Credit risk.
- Insurance and risk management.
- Financial regulation.
- Development economics.
- African law and economics.
- Data governance and artificial intelligence.
- Structured finance and securitisation.
The telematics paper can also go to SSRN, particularly within insurance, actuarial science, risk management and machine-learning classifications.
9. Poe or PLOS ONE
- Poe.com: not a scholarly repository or peer-reviewed journal. It hosts AI applications and paper-search tools, so I would not treat posting there as academic publication.
- PLOS ONE: legitimate and potentially usable, but only after either paper contains original research results, reproducible analysis and supporting data or a defensible data-availability statement.
