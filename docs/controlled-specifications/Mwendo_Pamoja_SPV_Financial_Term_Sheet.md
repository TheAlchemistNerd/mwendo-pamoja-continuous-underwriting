# Mwendo Pamoja SPV Financial Term Sheet

**Status:** Preserved implementation documentation; illustrative and non-binding

**As of:** 24 August 2026

**Purpose:** Commercial basis for lender, legal, tax, accounting, servicing, data, and model diligence
**Precedence:** Executed finance documents prevail. Before execution, this term sheet controls financing assumptions used in the strategic memorandum, pricing paper, financial model, and lender presentation.

## 4. Asset Purchase and Eligibility

Each Purchased Receivable must satisfy eligibility at the purchase calculation time. Final criteria will include:

- valid and enforceable customer contract originated by an authorised entity;
- KES denomination and approved product type;
- verified customer, driver, vehicle, platform, policy, and account identifiers;
- required disclosures, consent, data rights, and collection authority;
- no prior sale, security, dispute, fraud flag, or prohibited set-off;
- product and policy status within approved parameters;
- arrears, remaining tenor, balance, and documentation within limits;
- complete point-in-time data and current servicing record; and
- compliance with platform, carrier, product, geographic, and concentration rules.

Ineligible balances, dilution, data defects, delinquency, carrier-refund uncertainty, and concentrations will be excluded or haircut. Purchase price may be at par, discount, or another agreed formula and must reconcile to cash consideration and receivable-level transfer data.

## 5. Borrowing Base, Advance Rate, and OC

The 75% Class A share is not the borrowing-base advance rate. The borrowing base will be calculated from eligible receivables by product, applying agreed advance rates, haircuts, reserves, and concentration deductions.

The debt-note OC ratio is:

$$
\text{OC ratio}
= \frac{\text{eligible receivables}}
{\text{Class A outstanding}+\text{Class B outstanding}}.
$$

The proposed minimum is 125%. Under the illustrative total-capital funding convention, USD 9.00 million equivalent divided by 75% implies USD 12.00 million equivalent of eligible receivables. At closing, USD 12.00 million divided by USD 8.10 million of Class A and B debt gives 148.15% debt-note OC.

The definitive borrowing-base certificate will identify source data, calculation date, product balances, exclusions, haircuts, concentrations, advances, debt, reserve, OC, deficiency, required cure, preparer, verifier, and dispute process.

## 6. Cash Reserve and Controlled Accounts

The cash reserve account target is three months of defined Class A and B debt service. The definition will state whether scheduled principal, interest, trustee cost, hedge payment, and backup-servicing cost are included.

Controlled accounts will include, as applicable:

- collection account;
- principal and finance-charge collection subaccounts;
- reserve account;
- receivable-purchase account;
- carrier premium or refund account; and
- distribution account.

Account agreements will define owner, bank, currency, permitted debit, signatory, daily sweep, permitted investment, bank replacement, statement access, reconciliation, and enforcement control. Reserve cash provides liquidity and is not described as an FX hedge.

## 7. Priority of Payments

### 7.1 Normal Waterfall

1. taxes and statutory, trustee, or security-agent costs that are legally senior;
2. primary and backup-servicing costs;
3. permitted hedge payments, if a hedge is used;
4. Class A interest;
5. Class A scheduled principal and required borrowing-base or covenant sweeps;
6. Class B interest, subject to agreed deferral provisions;
7. Class B scheduled principal and required sweeps;
8. reserve replenishment;
9. permitted receivable purchases during an active revolving period; and
10. Class C residual distribution, only when all distribution conditions are satisfied.

### 7.2 Early-Amortisation Waterfall

New purchases and residual distributions stop. After senior expenses, servicing, approved hedge payments, and Class A interest, available cash sweeps sequentially to Class A principal, then Class B interest and principal according to negotiated subordination. The enforcement waterfall will separately address security proceeds, hedge termination, and enforcement cost.

Definitive documents must classify principal collections, finance charges, fees, prepayments, insurance refunds, recoveries, reserve releases, hedge receipts, and liquidation proceeds.

## 8. Covenants and Triggers

| Test | Proposed threshold | Required definition and consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| OC | Minimum 125% | Product-level eligibility, cure, purchase stop, and cash sweep |
| Cash reserve | Three months of defined debt service | Replenishment and distribution block |
| NPL | 6.5% over an agreed rolling window | Early amortisation at or above the contractual threshold |
| Platform concentration | Maximum 25% | Defined against eligible or outstanding exposure; purchase restriction or haircut |
| Urban-geographic concentration | Maximum 15% | Defined classification and exposure measure; purchase restriction or haircut |
| Product and carrier concentration | To be negotiated | Limits reflecting product and counterparty risk |
| Data-quality trigger | To be negotiated | Score or purchase fallback, incident, and cure |
| PSI | 0.25 monitoring escalation | Investigation, challenger review, and possible contractual purchase suspension |

Each test will state numerator, denominator, population, window, frequency, source, calculation agent, verifier, warning, breach, cure, dispute, and consequence. PSI is a model-monitoring convention, not a statutory default by itself.

Early-amortisation events may include sustained covenant breach, excess delinquency or cumulative net loss, material servicer failure, loss of a key platform or carrier agreement, failure of controlled accounts, material data unavailability, hedge failure where relevant, and specified representation breaches.

Events of Default will separately include payment default, issuer insolvency, unenforceability or loss of security, material uncured representation or covenant breach, fraud, loss of required licence, and other negotiated lender protections. Voting, acceleration, cure, and enforcement rights will be stated by class.

## 9. Servicing and Operational Continuity

The servicer will maintain receivable-level contractual balances, cash allocation, arrears, default, cure, modification, complaint, collection, recovery, and reporting records. Material modifications and waivers require authority under an approved policy and finance documents.

Servicing provisions will include:

- standard of care and collections policy;
- customer notices, complaints, appeal, and hardship;
- daily cash transfer and monthly investor reporting;
- data lineage, retention, audit, and privacy controls;
- reconciliation to platform settlement, bank cash, borrowing base, and GL;
- servicer termination events and transition cooperation;
- backup-servicer data, credentials, testing, and activation; and
- business continuity, disaster recovery, cyber incident, and manual fallback.

## 10. Model, Policy, and Data Governance

The underwriting model produces real-world PD and uncertainty. The Credit Policy and Compliance Gate applies eligibility, affordability, exposure, insurance, data-quality, concentration, reserve, OC, NPL, fraud, and operational rules. Model, feature, and rule versions are separately approved and retained.

The SPV or lenders will receive approved reports on model performance, calibration, uncertainty, drift, missingness, fairness, overrides, interventions, incidents, and changes. A material model, feature, policy, product, data-source, platform, carrier, or servicer change requires notice and any consent defined in definitive documents.

## 11. Accounting, Regulatory, Tax, and ESG

The financial-asset holder applies IFRS 9 under an approved accounting policy. The carrier applies IFRS 17 to insurance contracts in its reporting perimeter. Lender regulatory capital treatment is determined by each regulated lender under applicable Kenyan rules and permissions. No IRB or other capital benefit is promised by this term sheet.

Closing is subject to Kenyan legal, regulatory, accounting, and tax advice on origination, receivable transfer, perfection, customer deductions, insurance distribution, data processing, fees, withholding, deductibility, and SPV tax status.

ESG and customer-protection reporting should measure insurance continuity, driver net cash effect, intervention receipt and outcome, pricing and limit distribution, complaints, appeals, road-safety indicators, and adverse effects. First-loss capital can absorb losses only through the defined waterfall. It does not guarantee that every intervention is costless to senior investors.

## 12. Conditions Precedent

- true-sale, perfection, non-consolidation, enforceability, regulatory, data, and tax opinions;
- required licences, consents, KYC, AML, and sanctions checks;
- funded Class C and cash reserve;
- approved portfolio tape and initial borrowing-base certificate;
- approved 36-month cohort and waterfall model;
- accounting policies and opening entries;
- privacy impact assessment, security testing, model validation, and fairness review;
- operational-readiness, backup-servicing, and disaster-recovery evidence; and
- hedge documentation if a currency mismatch is introduced.

## 13. Reporting and Audit Rights

Investor reporting will include borrowing base, collections, arrears, defaults, recoveries, concentrations, reserve, OC, waterfall, note balances, covenant status, model monitoring, material incidents, and financial statements. Reports will identify data cut-off, source, preparer, approver, correction, and audit trail.

Investors, the trustee, and authorised advisers will have proportionate audit and sample-testing rights, subject to confidentiality and data-protection controls. Personal data will be minimised or pseudonymised where detailed identity is unnecessary.
