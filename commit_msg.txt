Comprehensive Architectural Evolution, Model Governance, and Research Portfolio Expansion

This commit introduces a sweeping array of architectural updates, methodological enhancements, and a significant expansion of the research publication portfolio across the Mwendo Pamoja and Telematics Relativities frameworks. The scale of these changes touches upon deep Bayesian underwriting models, asymmetric copula structures, regulatory orchestration, and the introduction of full-fledged academic and industry submissions. This evolution ensures that both our theoretical foundations and production deployments remain mathematically rigorous, economically sound, and aligned with cutting-edge actuarial and machine learning standards.

### 1. Mwendo Pamoja: Credit Risk Architecture and Model Specifications

A central theme of this update is the reinforcement of the Credit Risk Model and Loss Architecture Specification, deeply integrating it across all parts of the Mwendo Pamoja documentation. This cross-pollination ensures that product states, timing definitions, and cash-loss components do not drift across the wider programme.

*   **Part 1: Product Architecture and Cascades:** We have introduced the conceptual framework of "Three credit clocks" between strain and realised loss. This includes the operating and decision clock, the contractual performance clock, and the loss and recovery clock. This separation prevents early liquidity warnings from being confused with contractual defaults or recovery timing, ensuring a mathematically precise representation of cash-flow resilience.
*   **Part 2a: Deep Temporal Representation and Feature Engineering:** A highly detailed "Credit-State Ledger and Five Clocks" section has been added. This establishes a strict separation between Event time (Te), Availability time (Ta), Decision time (Td), Label-maturity time (Tm), and Cash-realisation time (Tc). The feature store pattern is now strictly governed by these ledgers to guarantee availability-time correctness, eliminating look-ahead bias in our neural representations.
*   **Part 2b: Bayesian Underwriting and Asymmetric Copulas:** This section has seen a massive overhaul. The "B-Splines as Smooth Alternatives to Binning" section has been elevated to "Governed P-Splines for Nonlinear Liquidity Effects". Instead of relying merely on B-splines with AR(1) priors, the main specification now evaluates a moderately rich B-spline basis coupled with an explicit difference penalty (P-spline). This allows for sparse Gaussian updates in a hierarchical framework. Furthermore, a "Conditional Conjugacy for the Complete HLR" via Pólya-Gamma augmentation has been detailed, enabling efficient blocked Gibbs sampling offline. We also introduced a "Hierarchical Piecewise-Exponential Timing Challenger" to act as a survival model counterpart to the fixed-horizon HLR, offering insights into *when* defaults are expected to occur.
*   **Part 3, 4, 5, & 6 Integrations:** The regulatory orchestration, Enterprise ERP telemetry, KESONIA pricing, and implementation roadmaps have all been updated to explicitly reference the unified `CREDIT_RISK_MODEL_AND_LOSS_ARCHITECTURE_SPECIFICATION.md`. This ensures that downstream operations like ECL (Expected Credit Loss) handoffs, SPV funding, and waterfall logic are seamlessly connected to the upstream Bayesian posterior artifacts.
