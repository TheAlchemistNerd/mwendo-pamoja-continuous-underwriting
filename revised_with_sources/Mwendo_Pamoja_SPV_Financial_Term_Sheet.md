# Mwendo Pamoja Asset Financing SPV
## Indicative Financial Term Sheet (Phase 1 Pilot Facility)

**Confidential & Proprietary**
**Issuer:** Mwendo Pamoja Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)
**Originator & Servicer:** Mwendo Pamoja (Operating Company / HoldCo)
**Target Facility Size:** $9,000,000 USD (Approx. 1.17 Billion KES)

---

### 1. Facility Overview

*   **Structure:** Bankruptcy-Remote Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) issuing tranched Asset-Backed Notes.
*   **Eligible Assets:** Insurance Premium Financing (IPF) contracts and short-term working capital microloans originated to gig-economy drivers on vetted partner platforms (e.g., Oye Kenya, Uber, Bolt).
*   **Currency:** Mixed-currency facility (USD / KES) with structured FX risk hedging via The Currency Exchange Fund (TCX).
*   **Advance Rate:** 75% against eligible receivables.

### 2. Capital Tranching & Yield

The SPV capital stack is tranched to align with distinct risk-return mandates:

| Tranche | Size | % of SPV | Target Investor | Interest Rate / Yield | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class A (Senior Debt)** | $6,750,000 | 75% | Commercial Banks, DFIs | KES 91-Day T-Bill + 200 bps | First Priority |
| **Class B (Mezzanine)** | $1,350,000 | 15% | Structured Credit Funds | 18.0% - 22.0% Fixed (KES) | Second Priority |
| **Class C (First-Loss)** | $900,000 | 10% | Mwendo Pamoja HoldCo | Residual Net Margin | Subordinated |

*   **Maturity (Class A & B):** 12-Month Revolving Credit Facility, followed by a 6-month managed amortization / wind-down period. Interest paid monthly in arrears.

### 3. Credit Enhancement & Downside Protection

To protect Senior Lenders against localized defaults and systemic macroeconomic shocks, the facility incorporates physical financial buffers:

*   **Subordination:** Class A notes are protected by 25% total subordination (Class B + Class C).
*   **Overcollateralisation (OC):** The portfolio enforces a minimum structural Overcollateralisation ratio of 125%.
*   **Cash Reserve Account (CRA):** An active CRA funded at inception and replenished daily via API Split-Billing. The CRA is sized to cover 3 months of forward debt service on Class A and B notes.
*   **Historical Net Recovery Rate (NRR):** Modeled at 40% based on historical regional asset-finance data (physical track-and-trace repossession and insurance claim payouts).

### 4. Portfolio Covenants & Triggers

The SPV utilizes automated, algorithmic underwriting telemetry to enforce hard portfolio circuit breakers:

*   **Population Stability Index (PSI) Drift:** If the driver risk distribution drifts beyond a PSI threshold of 0.25 (indicating a structural market shift), new originations are automatically suspended pending model recalibration.
*   **Early Amortization Event:** Triggered immediately if the cumulative Net Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio exceeds **6.5%** over a rolling 30-day period. Upon triggering, the SPV enters mandatory liquidation mode: 100% of all incoming principal and interest collections are swept directly to amortize Class A notes.
*   **Concentration Limits:**
    *   Max 25% exposure to any single rideshare/gig platform ecosystem.
    *   Max 15% exposure within a single geographic urban cluster (Geohash).

### 5. Regulatory & ESG Alignment

*   **Accounting Standard:** Assets are held and provisioned strictly under **IFRS 9 (Financial Instruments)** utilizing a 3-Stage Expected Credit Loss (ECL) model driven by real-time cash flow telemetry.
*   **ESG Additionality:** The First-Loss Equity tranche absorbs the cost of all driver-facing leniencies (Premium Holidays, Micro-Reward Bridging), guaranteeing that ESG interventions never interrupt or dilute the Class A cash flow waterfall.
