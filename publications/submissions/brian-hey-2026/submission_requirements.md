- Accessed: 30 August 2026
- Local frozen copy: `2026-brian-hey-guidelines.docx`

## Confirmed requirements

- The 2026 submission window runs from 17 April to 31 August 2026.
- The guidance requests entries by the end of Monday, 31 August 2026.
- Entries are sent to the IFoA Communities Engagement team.
- Authors may be inside or outside the IFoA.
- The paper's contents should normally have been, or be planned to be, substantially covered at an IFoA event during the twelve months before and including the 2026 GIRO Conference.
- The entry must be accompanied by the date and name of that IFoA event.
- Papers wholly or partly funded through an IFoA research grant would not normally be considered.
- The submission should be self-contained: readers should understand the problem, form an initial view of the consequences and be able to implement the approach independently.
- Borrowed text and figures must be cited.
- The panel considers practical application, technical content, innovative content, readability and wider professional appeal. A material deficiency in practical application, technical content, innovative content or readability can prevent an award.

## Matters not specified in the downloaded guidance

The guidance does not prescribe a page limit, mandatory anonymisation, a fixed reference style, a submission portal, a precise end-of-day time, a mandatory file format, a prior-publication rule or a specific AI-assistance declaration. The submission edition uses a transparent AI-assistance statement and provides DOCX because the implementation plan locked a Word deliverable. These choices do not replace confirmation from the IFoA.

## Author action required before submission

1. Confirm the IFoA event at which the paper was or will be substantially covered, with the event name and date.
2. Obtain the current Communities Engagement submission email address from the IFoA and verify whether DOCX alone is accepted or whether a PDF should accompany it.
3. Confirm whether any IFoA research grant funded the work.
4. Review the AI-assistance declaration and amend it only if needed for factual completeness.
5. Review author affiliation, professional designations, acknowledgements and any conflict-of-interest statement that should accompany the entry.
6. Send the final manuscript and event indication before the deadline, retain the sent-email timestamp and preserve the submitted-file hashes.
