From platform activity to a financeable mobility model

What does it take to finance a vehicle-dependent livelihood responsibly?

CGAP’s work on gig-platform data helped connect that question to Moove’s operating context and to Mwendo Pamoja: vehicle uptime, driver earnings, insurance, repairs, payment schedules and the cash available after the costs of doing the work.

The accompanying article develops this into a practical research partnership. It follows the cash from completed work through platform settlement, operating costs and household needs, then tests how repair delays, insurance events and repayment timing affect driver resilience and portfolio performance.

The worked case keeps the ledgers distinct: driver cash, operator economics, insurance outcomes and financing cash flows each answer different questions. It shows why a repair intervention can raise driver surplus and reduce arrears while still changing the operator’s equity returns. Each effect needs its own measured evidence.

Underwrite for Collection supplies the lending discipline across the facility lifecycle: verify the repayment source, size payments to sustainable cash generation, monitor after disbursement and assess realized collections alongside the borrower’s ability to keep earning.

The research questions are practical:
• Which operational signals explain repayment capacity?
• Which support improves both earning continuity and repayment?
• How should risk and cash be allocated across drivers, platforms, insurers and funders?
