## Read the Moniepoint workflow

Five distinct workflow figures are supplied: one compact overview, one complete flowchart and three detailed panels. Twelve continuation images provide readable excerpts of the detailed panels for the Word supplement. Four new numerical charts accompany the transaction-to-decision case; the two earlier arithmetic figures are retained.

The article explains the evidence, calculations, deterministic checks, rolling cash measures, statistical uncertainty, human decisions, execution and monitoring around the figures. A rolling average is a changing measure calculated by a specified rule. Its observation window, cutoff, coverage requirement and formula are recorded separately from the policy threshold or review action.

The worked case implements a thirty-day trailing residual gate, source checks, a cash floor, posterior cash uncertainty, simulated maker-checker approvals and dated outcomes. The detailed flowchart identifies the experimental, causal, sequential-control and portfolio interfaces supplied by the five-article research series.
