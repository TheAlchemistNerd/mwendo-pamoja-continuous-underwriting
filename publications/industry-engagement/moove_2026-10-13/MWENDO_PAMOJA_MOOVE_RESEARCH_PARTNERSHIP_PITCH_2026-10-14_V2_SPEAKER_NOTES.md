## Slide 1
Purpose: open a focused discussion about a research and operating partnership. All modeled examples are clearly identified as synthetic unless stated otherwise.

## Slide 2
Anchor: CGAP, Putting Gig Data to Work (2023), pp. 4-5, as cited in the source package. This synthetic case sets out a specific mechanism for testing with partner operating records.

## Slide 3
The full model has four separate weekly ledgers, joined through dated events. The plotted scenarios are synthetic and use author-selected assumptions.

## Slide 4
Synthetic 156-week model outputs. Driver cash surplus: uninterrupted KES 468,000; scheduled repair KES 399,429; expedited repair KES 456,571; expedited repair with KES 25,000 fee credit KES 481,571. Household cash requirement is met in all four modeled paths.

## Slide 5
From the retained weekly ledgers: scheduled repair has 12 unavailable days, first arrears in week 13, peak arrears KES 50,000, cure in week 32, and KES 3.9 million collected over 156 weeks. Uninterrupted and expedited repair scenarios collect KES 3.9 million without modeled arrears.

## Slide 6
All values are synthetic outputs, not observed Moove outcomes. Scheduled repair requires KES 62,926 additional equity and has KES 50,000 peak arrears. Expedited repair requires KES 26,473 additional equity and has no modeled arrears. Fee credit requires KES 51,473 additional equity, with KES 25,000 waived fees.

## Slide 7
Synthetic fee-credit comparison. Driver cash surplus rises from KES 456,571 to KES 481,571, while the operator equity call rises from KES 26,473 to KES 51,473.

## Slide 8
The proposed trial assigns eligible repair cases to the current process or a specified expedited-response offer. If randomisation does not fit operations, a registered comparison design would document its identification assumptions.

## Slide 9
Apply Kenyan data-protection requirements and the participating institutions' governance. Operational events supply measurement evidence, while authorized teams retain credit decision authority.

## Slide 10
The workshop will define feasibility, data ownership, operating measures and approval gates for a scoped pilot.
